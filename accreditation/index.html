Accreditation and listings

Recognition

Recognized internationally

View JFK’s WDOMS listing (opens in a new tab)

World Directory of Medical Schools

JFK is listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS), developed by WFME and the Foundation for Advancement of International Medical Education and Research (FAIMER).

  • View JFK’s WDOMS listing(opens in a new tab)
AAEPO in WFME’s list of recognized agencies (opens in a new tab)

Accredited by AAEPO

JFK is accredited by the Agency for Accreditation of Educational Programs and Organizations (AAEPO), an accrediting agency recognized by the World Federation for Medical Education (WFME).

  • AAEPO in WFME’s list of recognized agencies(opens in a new tab)
Government of Curaçao (opens in a new tab)

Chartered by the Government of Curaçao

The Government of Curaçao, through the Ministry of Education, granted JFK a charter to operate a medical school on the island and to award the Doctor of Medicine degree.

  • Government of Curaçao(opens in a new tab)

ECFMG eligibility

According to WDOMS, JFK students and graduates are eligible to apply for ECFMG Certification, provided they meet all other ECFMG requirements. ECFMG Certification is required to enter U.S. graduate medical education and to take Step 3 of the USMLE. ECFMG lists JFK as eligible for Pathway 3 (a medical school accredited by an agency recognized by WFME), one of the ways recent graduates meet the clinical and communication skills requirements for certification.

  • See the ECFMG Sponsor Note(opens in a new tab)

After graduation

Licensing pathways

Every country sets its own licensing requirements. Graduates must meet the standards of the country where they wish to practice.

United States

Medical graduates must pass the three steps of the United States Medical Licensing Examination (USMLE), sponsored by the Federation of State Medical Boards and the National Board of Medical Examiners, before applying for a license to practice.

USMLE eligibility(opens in a new tab)

United Kingdom (PLAB)

Graduates of medical schools outside the UK usually take the two-part PLAB test, run by the General Medical Council (GMC), before applying for registration with a license to practice. The GMC assesses each qualification individually, so check your plans with the GMC directly.

GMC guide to the PLAB test(opens in a new tab)

Canada

According to the WDOMS listing, medical degrees from JFK are acceptable to the provincial and territorial medical regulatory authorities in Canada. Graduates must meet the licensing requirements of the Medical Council of Canada.

Canada note in JFK’s WDOMS listing(opens in a new tab)

India

India’s National Medical Commission (NMC), which replaced the Medical Council of India, licenses foreign medical graduates under its Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations. These set rules on course length, internship, language of instruction and the qualifying exam. Students who plan to practice in India should check the current NMC requirements before enrolling.

Guide for students from India
