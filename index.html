Start your journey in Curaçao.

Why JFK

Why study at JFK?

  • Small classes, personal teaching

    Small cohorts and one-on-one instruction in a comfortable learning environment.

  • Affordable tuition

    Low tuition and payment plans make a medical education within reach. See tuition and fees

  • No MCAT or entrance exam

    Applicants are assessed on their academic record, application materials and an interview.

  • Three intakes a year

    Start in Spring (January), Summer (May) or Fall (September).

  • Taught in English

    All courses in the Pre-M.D. and M.D. programs are taught in English.

  • A safe island setting

    Curaçao lies outside the hurricane belt, so your studies can continue year-round.

Academics

Your path to the M.D.

A five-year program in total, from Pre-M.D. through MD 11. Students with qualifying prior studies enter the M.D. program directly.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Pre-M.D. Program

    3 semesters · 16 weeks each

    For students who do not yet meet the M.D. entry requirements. Successful students progress automatically to MD 1.

    Learn more

  2. 02

    Step 2: Basic Sciences

    MD 1–4 · Curaçao

    Four semesters of basic medical sciences, from anatomy and physiology to pathology and pharmacology, on campus in Willemstad.

    Learn more

  3. 03

    Step 3: Review semester

    MD 5

    A review and pre-clinical semester that prepares students for clinical training.

    Learn more

  4. 04

    Step 4: Clinical Rotations

    MD 6–11 · 72 or 84 weeks

    Inpatient and outpatient rotations in the United States, the United Kingdom and Nepal, and clinical rotations in Curaçao.

    Learn more

Admissions

Apply in four steps

  1. 1Step 1

    Apply online

    Complete the online application, upload copies of your documents and pay the US$90 non-refundable application fee.

  2. 2Step 2

    Evaluation

    The admissions team evaluates your application and documents. If necessary, it also invites you to an interview by phone or in person.

  3. 3Step 3

    Final decision

    The admissions team gives you its final answer.

  4. 4Step 4

    Enroll and travel

    Accepted students receive an immigration information package to prepare for study in Curaçao.

FAQ

Questions?
JFK has answers.

Can't find what you're looking for? The JFK admissions team is happy to help.

Contact Admissions
When can students start?
Classes start three times a year: in Spring (January), Summer (May) and Fall (September). See the academic calendar.
Is the MCAT required?
No. The MCAT is not required. If you have taken it, you are welcome to include your score with your application.
Is there an entrance exam?
No. The admissions committee evaluates your academic record, essay and letters of recommendation, and interviews applicants if necessary. Read about student selection.
What is needed to enter the M.D. program directly?
College-level coursework of one year each in biology, physics, inorganic chemistry and organic chemistry. Students without these courses can start with the Pre-M.D. program. See all admission requirements.
Can graduates apply for ECFMG Certification?
Yes. According to the World Directory of Medical Schools, JFK students and graduates are eligible to apply for ECFMG Certification, provided they meet all other ECFMG requirements. ECFMG also lists JFK as eligible for Pathway 3 (a school accredited by a WFME-recognized agency), one of the ways recent graduates meet the clinical skills requirements. See the official ECFMG Pathways list (opens in a new tab), the WDOMS listing (opens in a new tab) and accreditation and recognition.
What language are classes taught in?
All classes are taught in English.
Where is the campus?
The JFK campus is at Pareraweg 45, Willemstad, Curaçao.
How can tuition be paid?
By wire transfer, credit or debit card, or PayPal (no cash). Payment plans are available and must be arranged before the first day of classes. See tuition and fees.

Ready to start your journey in medicine?

Classes begin in Spring (January), Summer (May) and Fall (September). The JFK admissions team is happy to answer your questions.
