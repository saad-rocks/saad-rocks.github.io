Admissions

Apply to JFK

Start in Spring (January), Summer (May) or Fall (September). The admissions office replies to every application within one week.

Before you start

What you will need

  • Your personal details and contact information
  • Program (Pre-M.D., M.D. Basic Sciences or M.D. Clinical Sciences) and preferred start term (Spring, Summer or Fall)
  • Schools attended, with dates, degrees and grades
  • Scanned transcripts, a passport-size photo and a copy of your passport or ID
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • The US$90 application fee, paid with PayPal (or a card through PayPal) after you submit

Copies of your documents are fine for your application, and nothing needs to be mailed. Bring all originals with you when you arrive in Curaçao.
