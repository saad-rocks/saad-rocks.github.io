Student Selection

A holistic review of every applicant, with no entrance examination.

The process

How applicants are selected

  1. 1

    Application

    You submit your application with academic transcripts, letters of recommendation, a personal essay and the other required documents.

  2. 2

    Review of written materials

    The admissions committee reviews your academic record, essay and letters, looking for academic achievement, community contributions, work or volunteer experience and the qualities that make you stand out.

  3. 3

    Interview, if necessary

    If the admissions team needs to know more, it invites you to an interview by phone or in person.

  4. 4

    Final decision

    The admissions team gives its final answer, based on all application materials, any interview and your overall fit with JFK’s values and goals.

If you are interviewed

What an interview looks at

Interviews are held only when the admissions team needs to know more about an applicant.

  • Demeanor

    Interpersonal skills, communication and professional conduct.

  • Educational level

    Your academic background, coursework and preparedness for the program.

  • Commitment

    Your goals and your commitment to medicine and to JFK.

  • Motivation and perseverance

    Your passion for medicine and your ability to work through challenges.

Your application

Make a strong case

The committee looks at your character and commitment as well as your grades.

  • Personal essay

    Share your experiences, values and goals.

  • Letters of recommendation

    Strong endorsements of your abilities and character.

  • Work or volunteer experience

    Practical skills and commitment to the field.

  • Community contributions

    Social responsibility beyond academics.

There is no entrance examination. Selection is based on your application materials and, if held, an interview.

Ready to start your journey in medicine?

Classes begin in Spring (January), Summer (May) and Fall (September). The JFK admissions team is happy to answer your questions.
