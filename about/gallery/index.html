White Coat Ceremony 2026

Welcomed into medicine by Curaçao's Minister of Education

At the 2026 White Coat Ceremony, Curaçao's Minister of Education was the chief guest and presented new JFK students with their white coats. Students then took their oath together, marking their first step toward becoming physicians.
